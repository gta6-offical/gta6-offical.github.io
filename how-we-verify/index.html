How we verify GTA 6 facts

Every claim on this site carries an implied label: confirmed, reported or unknown. This page explains the method so you can judge for yourself.

Updated: November 14, 2026

GTA 6 attracts rumours, fake leaks and recycled headlines. We use a simple, repeatable method to keep facts apart from guesses. You can apply the same method to any page about the game, including ours.

Our three labels

Evidence labels used on this site
LabelMeaningExample
ConfirmedStated on an official Rockstar page or in a Take-Two filing that we read directlyRelease date of 19 November 2026
ReportedPublished by a news outlet; not checked against an official page; written with attributionUS$79.99 and US$99.99 price points
UnknownNot announced, or only seen in snippets; we do not state it as factPC version, PEGI rating

Source order

  1. Rockstar's official page, rockstargames.com/VI, for the game's name, date, platforms, editions, setting and rating label.
  2. Take-Two filings and earnings releases on the SEC site for business statements, such as the 9 November 2026 reaffirmation of the date.
  3. Established news outlets for details that the first two do not cover, always with attribution.
  4. Everything else is treated as unverified and kept out of the text.

What we do not publish

  • Leaks, stolen footage or datamined content.
  • Map sizes, system requirements, review scores or sales numbers that nobody official has released.
  • PC dates, PS5 Pro details or PEGI numbers without a primary source.
  • Piracy, cracks, "free download" links, cheats for online play or gambling content.

The list of open items is kept on what is still unknown.

A worked example

Take the price. Several outlets report US$79.99 for Standard and US$99.99 for Ultimate. We have not seen the figures on an official store page. So we write "reportedly" and tell readers to check the store, as on editions explained. Compare the release date: it is on Rockstar's page and in Take-Two's 8-K, so we write it as a plain fact.

Another example is the unlock time. A site claims a specific global hour. Rockstar has not published one, and storefronts reportedly show regional midnight. We say exactly that and no more.

Why summaries need care

Some pages we use are long or only partly available to us, and automated summaries can garble details. When our notes flag a risk, such as the middle step of the delay history, we attribute the claim and avoid stating more than the sources support. This is why the delay history in why GTA 6 matters says "according to reports".

Dates and updates

Every page is written as of 14 November 2026. We try to phrase things so they remain accurate after launch, for example "release date: 19 November 2026" rather than "coming in five days". When a fact changes, the page changes, and the glossary and unknowns pages are updated first because they gather the most-searched questions.

General background is labelled too

Series history, such as the years of earlier games on the history page, is long-established public knowledge and is not part of the GTA VI fact sheet. We say this on the page itself.

How to check a GTA 6 claim yourself

  1. Who said it, and is it Rockstar or Take-Two?
  2. Is there a date and a link to the primary page?
  3. Is it a fact, a forecast or an opinion?
  4. Do two independent outlets cite the same primary source, or just each other?
  5. Does it include a specific number nobody has released, such as a map size?

If you find an error on this site, tell the site owner. We correct mistakes and note meaningful changes.

For parents, the same method applies to age ratings; see ratings and parental guidance. Details about who we are: about this site. Terms are defined in the glossary.

Limits of our method

No method is perfect. We read official pages and filings, but some pages are long, and some details may change after we read them. We also cannot check store prices in every country. That is why many entries tell you to confirm on the store, and why reported items are always attributed. Where we are unsure, we say so and show the item on the unknowns list. We would rather publish a short, honest answer than a long, impressive guess.

Our editorial team is independent, and no company has paid for or reviewed our pages. The site carries no adverts for downloads or key sellers.

Frequently asked questions

How do you decide what is confirmed?

A fact is confirmed only if it is on Rockstar’s official page or in a Take-Two filing that we read directly.

What does 'reported' mean?

It means a news outlet published it and we have not checked it against an official page, so we attribute it.

Do you publish GTA 6 leaks?

No. We do not publish leaks, datamined content, or unverified numbers.

Why do you not state a PEGI rating?

We have not verified it on an official PEGI source, so it stays listed as unknown.

How often is the site updated?

Pages are written as of 14 November 2026 and updated when official sources change.

Which source do you trust most?

Rockstar’s official page, followed by Take-Two’s SEC filings.

How can I report a mistake?

Contact the site owner. We correct errors and update the page.

Are you affiliated with Rockstar Games?

No. We are an independent fan editorial team, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
