How the GTA series got to GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto is nearly three decades old. This page is a short history for newcomers, from the first top-down game to GTA VI.

Updated: November 14, 2026

You do not need to have played earlier games to enjoy a new Grand Theft Auto, because each main entry tells a separate story. Still, knowing the history explains why GTA VI is such a big deal and why Vice City sounds familiar.

Note on sourcing: the release years below are long-established general history, not items from the GTA VI fact sheet we use for news. GTA VI details elsewhere on this site are checked separately. See how we verify facts.

The main games in order

Main Grand Theft Auto games and what each is known for

Grand Theft Auto1997Top-down view, the original crime sandbox
Grand Theft Auto III2001First fully 3D open world in the series
Grand Theft Auto: Vice City20021980s Miami-style city, strong radio and music identity
Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas2004A whole state with several cities
Grand Theft Auto IV2008More realistic tone, set in Liberty City

Grand Theft Auto VI19 November 2026Leonida, Vice City, Jason and Lucia

There were other releases, including expansions and handheld spin-offs, but the table lists the games most people mean by "the series". Each main game has a self-contained plot, so you can start with GTA VI.

What stays the same in every game

  • An open world: a large city or region you can explore freely, on foot or in vehicles.
  • Story missions plus side activities: a main plot you can follow in order, and optional things to do.
  • Satire of modern life: radio, adverts and television inside the game poke fun at real culture.
  • A mature rating: violence, strong language and adult themes. Details in ratings and parental guidance.

What GTA V changed

GTA V (2013) let players switch between three protagonists and later added GTA Online, a separate multiplayer mode that has been updated for years. Outlets report that GTA V reached 225 million units shipped by the December 2025 quarter, and the franchise more than 465 million. That success is why the follow-up took so long and why expectations are so high. The business side is covered on why GTA 6 matters.

Where Vice City fits

Vice City was the setting of the 2002 game Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. GTA VI uses the name again as the main city of the state of Leonida, and Rockstar's tagline for the new game is "Vice City, USA. The darkest side of the sunniest place in America." This is a new game with new characters, not a remake. Rockstar has not confirmed any story connection to the 2002 plot, so treat "connections" in fan videos as theories.

What is different in GTA VI, as far as we can confirm

Two protagonists

Jason and Lucia are confirmed. GTA V had three, earlier games usually had one.

A return to Vice City

The city is confirmed as the main location inside the state of Leonida.

New hardware

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only, as of 14 November 2026.

Single-player focus

Reported as a single-player experience, with no online mode announced.

Do I need to play older games first?

No. Rockstar builds each main entry as a separate story. If you want a taste of the series first, GTA V is the most recent, but this is a personal choice rather than a requirement. We do not give advice on where to get older games beyond the official stores.

Terms you will meet while catching up

Words such as "open world", "sandbox", "radio station" and "Online" appear in every discussion. They are explained briefly in the GTA glossary.

How the series is usually described by its critics and fans

Fans praise the scale and detail of the worlds. Critics often point to the controversy around violence and the way each game satirises society. Both views are part of the series story, and each new game revives the debate. We do not give review scores for GTA VI because the game is not yet reviewed in the sources we use.

What to watch for next

After launch, people will want to compare GTA VI with GTA V. Comparisons about map size, number of missions or hours of play need official numbers, and none has been published by Rockstar. When that changes, the unknowns page will be where the answers move from "unknown" to "confirmed". For a general overview, go back to the GTA 6 explainer, or check the official GTA VI site.

A quick way to remember the eras

  • Top-down era (1997): the original game, seen from above.
  • 3D era (2001 to 2004): Grand Theft Auto III, Vice City and San Andreas made the series what most people recognise.
  • HD era (2008 to 2013): Grand Theft Auto IV and V brought modern graphics and online play.
  • Current era (2026): Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

These labels are our own shorthand for readers, not official terms. If you only remember one thing, remember that every main game stands alone, and the long gap before GTA VI is the exception rather than the rule in the series' history.

Frequently asked questions

How many main GTA games are there?

The main entries are Grand Theft Auto (1997), III (2001), Vice City (2002), San Andreas (2004), IV (2008), V (2013) and VI (19 November 2026).

Do I need to play GTA V before GTA 6?

No. Each main game has its own story, so you can start with GTA VI.

When did GTA V come out?

GTA V was released in 2013. It has been the latest main game until GTA VI.

Is GTA 6 a Vice City remake?

No. It uses Vice City as the main city in the new state of Leonida with new protagonists, Jason and Lucia.

What is GTA Online?

It is the multiplayer mode of GTA V, launched after that game. It is separate from GTA VI, and no GTA VI online mode has been announced.

Which GTA game sold the most?

Outlets report GTA V at 225 million units shipped as of the December 2025 quarter, and the series at over 465 million.

Who makes the GTA games?

Rockstar Games develops them and Take-Two Interactive publishes them.
