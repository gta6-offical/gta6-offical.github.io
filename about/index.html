About this site

This is an unofficial, independent fan site that explains Grand Theft Auto VI in plain English.

Updated: November 14, 2026

This site is run by an independent fan editorial team. It is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names are trademarks of their owners.

What we do

We explain GTA 6 for people who have not followed it: what it is, the history, editions, ratings, online status and open questions. Start with the main explainer or read how we verify facts.

Sources policy

Official Rockstar and Take-Two sources come first. Reports from outlets are attributed, and unverified claims are left out. Facts are current as of 14 November 2026. See what is still unknown. Official site: rockstargames.com/VI.

Privacy

  • The hosting server keeps ordinary server logs.
  • We use Ahrefs Web Analytics, which gives aggregate statistics without cookies.
  • We set no cookies of our own and have no accounts or comment forms.
  • We do not sell or collect personal data.

Contact

Contact is via the site owner. We do not publish an email address on the site. For corrections, mention the page and the claim.

Please note

We do not offer downloads, cracks, cheats for online play or gambling content, and we do not link to any. For game information on buying, see editions explained; for families, ratings and parental guidance.
