GTA glossary: terms you will see in GTA 6 news

GTA coverage is full of shorthand. This glossary explains the words in plain English and points to the right page for more.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    News about Grand Theft Auto VI uses a lot of jargon from gaming and business. Each term below gets a short definition. Where a term refers to a GTA 6 fact, we say whether it is confirmed.

    The game, the series and the setting

    Core terms

    Terms about the game
    TermMeaning
    GTA 6 / GTA VIShort forms of Grand Theft Auto VI, the next main game, released 19 November 2026.
    Rockstar GamesThe studio that develops the games. Rockstar is a label of Take-Two.
    Take-Two InteractiveThe publisher and parent company, which reports earnings to investors.
    LeonidaThe fictional US state where GTA VI is set. Confirmed.
    Vice CityThe main city of the game; the name also belongs to the 2002 game Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.
    Jason and LuciaThe two confirmed protagonists. Reported full names: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
    ProtagonistA main character the player controls.
    MainlineA numbered main game, as opposed to a spin-off.
    Spin-offA side game that is not a numbered entry.
    Open worldA game map you can freely explore instead of following fixed levels.
    SandboxA game that gives you freedom to choose what to do.
    MissionA story task with a goal, as opposed to free exploration.
    Side activityOptional content not required to finish the story.
    Radio stationIn-game radio with music, talk and adverts. Confirmed as a feature.
    Wanted levelA series mechanic where police respond to crimes. Not detailed for GTA VI.
    NPCA non-player character, controlled by the game.

    The series history shows where each of these ideas started.

    Places and real-world references

    • Liberty City, San Andreas, Vice City: the three cities of the 1997 original; each returned in later games.
    • Los Santos: the city of GTA V, a fictional take on Los Angeles.
    • Real-world inspiration: a real place that a fictional one resembles. Leonida is widely read as a take on Florida; see Leonida's Florida roots.
    • Vice City Stories: a 2006 prequel set in Vice City in 1984, first released on PSP.

    Buying, setup and safety

    Buying and release

    Terms about buying and release
    TermMeaning
    Standard editionThe base version of the game. Confirmed as a name.
    Ultimate editionThe higher-priced version. Confirmed as a name; contents not verified by us.
    Vintage Vice City PackThe pre-order bonus name.
    Pre-orderBuying before release to secure a copy or bonus.
    PreloadDownloading files before release; the game stays locked until the unlock time.
    Unlock timeThe moment the game becomes playable. Not announced globally.
    Day-one patchAn update released on launch day that fixes problems.
    DelayA move to a later release date. GTA VI had delays, per reports.
    Gone goldIndustry phrase meaning the game is finished for manufacture.
    Digital codeA key to redeem a game on a console store. Reportedly in boxed copies.
    StorefrontAn online shop, such as the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.
    GTA+Rockstar's subscription service. Its link to GTA VI is unverified.
    Order confirmationThe receipt from the store; keep it until the game runs. See the preload guide.

    The purchase side is covered by editions explained.

    Console setup terms

    Terms about storage, accounts and security
    TermMeaning
    SSDSolid-state drive, the fast internal storage in PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
    Expansion storageExtra fast storage: an M.2 drive on PS5 or a Storage Expansion Card on Xbox Series X|S.
    Rest modeA low-power state in which the console can keep downloading.
    Two-step verificationA second login check, such as a code, that protects an account.
    PhishingA fake message or page built to steal logins or payment details.

    Practical steps are on console preparation and avoiding scams.

    Ratings and safety

    Terms about ratings and safety
    TermMeaning
    ESRBEntertainment Software Rating Board, the rating body for the US and Canada. GTA VI shows Mature.
    PEGIPan European Game Information, the European age rating system. Not verified for GTA VI.
    Content descriptorA label that says why a game got its rating.
    In-game purchasesOptional paid extras inside a game.
    Parental controlsConsole settings that restrict games, spending and time.
    Family accountA linked account structure used to manage children's profiles.

    Online, platforms and labels

    Online and hardware

    Terms about modes and hardware
    TermMeaning
    Single-playerA game you play alone. GTA VI is reported as one.
    MultiplayerPlay with others online or locally. None announced for GTA VI.
    GTA OnlineThe online mode of GTA V.
    Co-opPlayers team up rather than compete.
    PS5, Xbox Series X|SThe two confirmed platforms.
    PS5 ProA stronger PS5 model. No GTA VI enhancements confirmed.
    Last-genOlder consoles, PS4 and Xbox One. Not announced.
    PortA version adapted for another platform, such as PC.

    Short phrases you may meet in comments

    • "Hype train": fast-growing excitement around a release.
    • "Leak": information that escaped before an official announcement. We do not publish leaks.
    • "Datamine": digging through game files for hidden information, often unreliable.
    • "Copium": slang for wishful thinking, common in comment sections.
    • "Launch window": the planned period for a release.

    Slang changes quickly; these entries describe general internet use, not Rockstar terms.

    Verification terms used on this site

    • Confirmed: stated on an official Rockstar page or a Take-Two filing.
    • Reported: published by a news outlet, not checked against an official page.
    • Unknown: not announced; shown on what is still unknown.
    • Speculation: a guess, which we do not publish as fact.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1What does GTA VI stand for?

    Grand Theft Auto VI, written with the Roman numeral six. GTA 6 is the same game.

    Q2What is Leonida?

    The fictional US state in which GTA VI takes place, with Vice City as its main city.

    Q3What does preload mean?

    Downloading the game before release. It stays locked until the unlock time.

    Q4What is an open world game?

    A game with a large map you can explore freely rather than a fixed series of levels.

    Q5What is ESRB?

    The Entertainment Software Rating Board, which rates games in the US and Canada. GTA VI shows Mature.

    Q6What is the difference between GTA Online and GTA 6?

    GTA Online is the multiplayer mode of GTA V. No online mode for GTA VI has been announced.

    Q7What is a day-one patch?

    An update released on launch day to fix problems. Rockstar has not described one for GTA VI.

    Q8What does 'reported' mean on this site?

    A claim from a news outlet that we have not verified against an official source.
