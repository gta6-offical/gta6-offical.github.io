What we still do not know about GTA 6

Rockstar shares little. This page lists the open questions as of 14 November 2026, so you can spot rumours that pretend to answer them.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    A good way to avoid misinformation is to know where the gaps are. The table below lists questions people search for and the honest status of each one. If a site gives a firm answer to an item marked unknown, ask for the official source.

    Why unknowns are normal

    Many of these items are answered only by a store listing, a patch note or a rating board publication. For a game of this size such details often appear close to release or after it. An empty answer is not a sign of a problem; it means the publisher has not spoken yet. Rockstar has also historically kept details back until close to launch. The correct move is to wait for the primary source rather than accept a confident guess.

    The gaps, topic by topic

    Platforms: PC, PS4 and PS5 Pro

    The only confirmed platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. People often cite "PC in 2027" or "PC in 2028", but these are speculation and we do not publish them. A PC announcement would come from Rockstar. A PS5 Pro mention appears at only one outlet and is not backed by an official enhancement list. Any website offering a "GTA 6 PC download" is therefore fake, as explained in how to avoid GTA 6 scams.

    Content and rating details

    The ESRB descriptors are known, but PEGI has not been verified. Read ratings and parental guidance for what to do meanwhile. We also do not know what the "in-game purchases" label refers to, or which in-game accessibility options the game offers; general console settings are covered in our accessibility guide.

    Gameplay and world details

    Rockstar's page confirms two protagonists, radio stations and an open world centred on Vice City. Trailers have shown other things, such as wildlife and social-media parodies, but we did not re-verify them against an official source, so we make no detailed claims about them. Names of other regions outside Vice City appear in outlets; we have not confirmed them, so this site uses only Leonida and Vice City. The real places that inspired the setting are a separate matter, described as real-world geography on Leonida's Florida roots. No map size comparison with GTA V has been published.

    Release logistics

    The release date, 19 November 2026, is confirmed. Less certain are the unlock time in your region and the preload start of 12 November 2026, which is reported by one outlet. Rockstar has not announced one global time, so check your store page rather than a social media post. What to do on the day is in the pre-order and preload guide.

    Business details

    Take-Two reaffirmed the date on 9 November 2026 and kept its net bookings guidance at US$8.0 to 8.2 billion. It did not publish pre-order counts or sales forecasts for the game itself. The context is in why GTA 6 matters.

    How questions leave this page

    1. Rockstar or Take-Two publishes an answer on an official page or in a filing.
    2. We check the primary source and note the date.
    3. The item moves to "confirmed" on the relevant page, with the date in the text.

    Reports from outlets stay labelled reported until a primary source exists; the labels are explained in how we verify facts.

    This list reflects our sources as of 14 November 2026. Some answers may already exist on pages we did not read, so check Rockstar's page first.

    Claims to treat with caution after launch

    After 19 November 2026, new claims will multiply: review scores, sales records, hours played, secrets. Wait for named outlets or Take-Two filings. Early "record sales" posts without a named source are marketing talk, not data. The glossary decodes the jargon in such posts.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1Is there a GTA 6 PC release date?

    No. A PC version has not been announced as of 14 November 2026.

    Q2Does GTA 6 have a PS5 Pro version?

    No official PS5 Pro details were found. Rockstar’s page reportedly says the game plays best on PS5.

    Q3What is the GTA 6 PEGI rating?

    Not verified. The ESRB rating shown on Rockstar’s page is Mature.

    Q4How big is the GTA 6 map?

    Rockstar has not published a map size. Any figure online is a guess.

    Q5How many hours is GTA 6?

    Story length has not been announced.

    Q6What is the GTA 6 file size?

    We could not verify a file size. Check your store listing before preloading.

    Q7What time does GTA 6 unlock?

    Rockstar has not announced a single global unlock time. Your store listing is the best guide for your region.

    Q8Is there a GTA 6 beta or demo?

    None has been announced. Offers of beta access are scams.
