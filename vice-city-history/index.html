World & Series

Vice City through the years

Vice City is the oldest recurring city in the series after Liberty City. Here is how it appeared before, and what is confirmed about its return in GTA 6.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    When Rockstar revealed that Grand Theft Auto VI returns to Vice City, long-time players recognised the name at once. This page traces the city through earlier games and separates what is known about the new version from fan assumptions. The game history here is long-established public knowledge, not part of our GTA VI fact sheet.

    Vice City before GTA 6

    Vice City has appeared in several forms. Each version is a separate creation, built for its own game and era, so knowing one does not tell you the layout of another.

    1997: one of three cities

    The original Grand Theft Auto had three fictional cities: Liberty City, San Andreas and Vice City. All three later became settings for whole games. In 1997 the city was a top-down grid of streets, and the Miami flavour was mostly in the name and the sunny palette.

    2002: Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

    Vice City arrived on PlayStation 2 in 2002, with PC and Xbox versions after it. It moved the series to 1986 and followed Tommy Vercetti, a criminal building an empire in a neon city of beaches, pastel buildings and speedboats. Its licensed 1980s soundtrack and radio stations became one of the most remembered parts of the series. For the wider sequence of games, see the GTA series history.

    2006: Vice City Stories

    Vice City Stories was released for the PlayStation Portable in 2006 and came to PlayStation 2 the following year. A prequel set in 1984, it followed Victor Vance, a character first seen in the 2002 game. It showed that the setting could carry more than one story.

    2021: the Definitive Edition

    In 2021 Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy, The Definitive Edition, a remaster of GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas. It brought the 2002 Vice City to current hardware of the time, so many younger players met the city there rather than on PS2.

    What inspired the city

    The real-world model is Miami, Florida. This is the commonly accepted reading of the city's beaches, art deco hotels and Latin American influences, and the 2002 game leaned heavily on 1980s pop culture.

    Miami in the 1980s, as pop culture saw it

    Critics and fans have long linked the 2002 game to Miami-set crime television and films of the 1980s: pastel suits, fast boats, synth music and a city portrayed as glamorous and corrupt at once. Those links are interpretation by commentators, not statements we have from Rockstar, and they describe a screen image of Miami rather than the real city. The real geography that also shapes the new state is covered in Leonida's real Florida roots.

    Beaches and hotels

    Ocean-front strips with pastel art deco buildings, modelled on Miami Beach.

    Music and radio

    In-game radio was central in 2002. Radio stations are confirmed again for GTA VI.

    Satire

    Adverts, talk shows and TV mock the excess of the era being depicted.

    Vice City in GTA 6

    Rockstar's tagline for the new game is "Vice City, USA. The darkest side of the sunniest place in America." Vice City is confirmed as the main city of the state of Leonida, and Jason and Lucia are the confirmed protagonists. That is the solid core.

    New game, not a remake

    GTA VI is a new game with new characters. Rockstar has not described it as a remake of the 2002 game or as a sequel to its plot, and it has not confirmed any story link to Tommy Vercetti or Victor Vance. Fan videos about "connections" are theories. The same caution applies to the layout: Rockstar has not published a map or a size comparison with the old city.

    What we do not state

    • No map size or district list, because Rockstar has not published one we verified.
    • No claims about returning characters, landmarks or businesses from earlier games.
    • No time period: Rockstar has not given an official year for the setting.
    • Other region names in Leonida, which Rockstar's site lists but we have not verified.

    Old games and the new one share a name, not a map. Treat any "same street, same building" comparison as fan analysis until Rockstar shows otherwise.

    If you want to visit the old city first

    You do not need to play the 2002 game to follow GTA VI. If you are curious, buy older games only through official stores; "free download" offers are covered in how to avoid GTA 6 scams. Unfamiliar terms such as "remaster" are in the GTA glossary, open questions about the new map are on what is still unknown, and the official reference is rockstargames.com/VI. For the basics of the new game, start at the GTA 6 explainer.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1Is GTA 6 set in Vice City?

    Yes. Vice City is confirmed as the main city of the state of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto VI.

    Q2Is GTA 6 a remake of GTA Vice City?

    No. It is a new game with new protagonists, Jason and Lucia. Rockstar has not called it a remake or a sequel to the 2002 plot.

    Q3When did GTA Vice City come out?

    Grand Theft Auto: Vice City was released on PlayStation 2 in 2002, with PC and Xbox versions afterwards. It is set in 1986.

    Q4What real city is Vice City based on?

    Vice City is commonly understood as a fictional version of Miami, Florida.

    Q5Will Tommy Vercetti appear in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not announced any returning characters, so treat such claims as fan theories.

    Q6How many games are set in Vice City?

    Vice City appears in the 1997 original, Vice City (2002), Vice City Stories (2006), the 2021 remaster of the 2002 game, and GTA VI.

    Q7Is the GTA 6 Vice City the same map as the old one?

    Rockstar has not published a map or comparison. The new city is built for a new game, so do not assume the layouts match.
