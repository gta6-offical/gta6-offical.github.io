GTA 6 editions explained: what you actually get

Rockstar names two editions, Standard and Ultimate, and a pre-order bonus. Prices and extras are mostly reported, so here is what is solid and what to check.

Updated: November 14, 2026

Buying a big game can be confusing. This page explains the GTA 6 editions in plain terms, separates official names from reported details and lists what to check before you pay.

The short answer

Rockstar's official page names two editions of Grand Theft Auto VI, Standard and Ultimate, and a pre-order bonus called the Vintage Vice City Pack. Outlets report prices of US$79.99 for Standard and US$99.99 for Ultimate. We have not seen those prices on an official store page, so use them as a guide only. The game releases on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The honest summary is that Rockstar confirms the names, while the contents of each edition and the exact prices should be read from the store you plan to use. We do not list extra items for Ultimate because we could not verify them. Prices in other countries and currencies are not covered by our sources.

The Vintage Vice City Pack

The Vintage Vice City Pack is the confirmed name of the pre-order bonus. According to The National's report, the bonus includes a 1955 Vapid Stanier car, outfits and weapon designs, and was offered for orders placed before 20 November. A report from a single aggregator also mentions one month of GTA+ with a digital pre-order; we treat that as unverified and you should not rely on it. Because the pre-order window may be over by the time you read this, check whether the pack is still offered.

Pre-order and preload

  • Pre-orders: reportedly opened on 25 June 2026 through the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store and retailers.
  • Preload: reportedly starts on 12 November 2026 for digital copies, so you can download before launch.
  • Unlock time: storefronts reportedly show a regional midnight release, but Rockstar has not announced one global time. Check your store listing.

"Preload" means downloading the game files ahead of release. The game stays locked until the unlock time. See the glossary for that and similar terms.

Physical or digital?

The National reports that the boxed copy contains a download code rather than a game disc. That is a report we could not confirm on a store page, so read the product description before you buy a boxed copy. If you want a physical item, check what is inside the box. If you prefer digital, buy directly from the console store so the game is tied to your account.

Is there a PC version, a demo or a PS4 version?

No PC version, demo, beta, PlayStation 4 or Xbox One version has been announced as of 14 November 2026. PS5 Pro details are also missing: Rockstar's page reportedly says the game plays best on PlayStation 5, but we found no official enhancement list. These gaps are tracked on what is still unknown.

Buying checklist

  1. Confirm your console: PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S.
  2. Open the official store listing and read the edition name and price in your currency.
  3. Check what the listing says about the pre-order bonus and the unlock time.
  4. Make sure you have enough free storage before preload; Rockstar has not published a file size we could verify.
  5. Check the age rating and spending settings if the console is shared with a child, as explained in ratings and parental guidance.

Warning: keys sold at suspiciously low prices, "free download" offers and anything asking for your console login are scams or violate store terms. This site does not link to such offers. Use official storefronts.

Does the edition affect online play?

No GTA VI online mode has been announced, so no edition is described as unlocking one. In-game purchases are mentioned in the rating label, but Rockstar has not explained what they are. See single-player vs online.

Verdict: if you only want the game and are unsure, start from Standard and read the official listing for what Ultimate adds. Do not pay extra on the basis of rumours. Official information is at rockstargames.com/VI.

What to do if the price differs

Store prices vary by country, tax and currency, and may change over time. If the price you see differs from the reported US figures, trust the store. A big discount on a brand-new, unreleased game from an unofficial seller is a warning sign. Retailers also differ in what they include with a pre-order, so read the small print on the product page and keep the order confirmation until you have the game working.
