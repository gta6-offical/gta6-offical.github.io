The real Florida behind Leonida

Leonida is a fictional state. This guide describes the real Florida it is modelled on, so you can tell real-world geography apart from confirmed game content.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    Rockstar's tagline calls Vice City "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America", and the state of Leonida is widely read as a fictional Florida. Everything in the real-world sections below describes actual Florida. None of it is a claim about what the game contains.

    Read this first: the only game places we have verified are the state of Leonida and Vice City. Rockstar's website lists other regions, but we have not confirmed their names, so we do not repeat them. Check Rockstar's official page for the current list.

    Real places that shape the picture

    Florida is a long peninsula with a dense coastal south, huge wetlands inside, and a chain of islands trailing into the sea. Each of these landscapes is part of what people mean when they say "a Florida setting".

    Miami, Miami Beach and Ocean Drive

    Miami is the model most people name for Vice City. Across Biscayne Bay, Miami Beach holds the Art Deco Historic District, a concentration of streamlined pastel buildings from the 1930s and 1940s that was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1979. Ocean Drive, facing the beach, is its best-known strip. The look appeared in the 2002 Vice City too, as explained in Vice City then and now.

    The Everglades and Lake Okeechobee

    West of the city lie the Everglades, a slow-moving "river of grass" draining south from Lake Okeechobee. Everglades National Park was established in 1947 and protects marshes, mangrove coasts and airboat country that feels nothing like downtown Miami. The contrast between glittering coast and swampy interior is a big part of Florida's identity.

    The Florida Keys and the Overseas Highway

    The Keys are a chain of coral islands curving southwest from the mainland to Key West. The Overseas Highway, part of US Route 1, links them with a series of bridges, the most famous being the Seven Mile Bridge. Fishing towns, marinas and long causeways over turquoise water define the area.

    Culture, climate and wildlife

    A Latin American gateway

    Miami has deep ties to Cuba and the wider Caribbean and Latin America. Little Havana, with Calle Ocho at its centre, is the best-known Cuban-American neighbourhood, and Spanish is widely spoken across the metro area. PortMiami is often called the cruise capital of the world, and the region is a hub for trade and air travel to the south.

    Sun, storms and humidity

    South Florida has a tropical climate with a hot, humid wet season and a milder dry season. The Atlantic hurricane season officially runs from 1 June to 30 November, and storms are a routine part of life on the coast. The "sunniest place in America" line plays on this reputation for sunshine.

    Real Florida wildlife

    • American alligators live in freshwater across the state, from lakes to golf-course ponds.
    • American crocodiles share southern Florida with alligators, one of the few places both live together.
    • West Indian manatees gather in warm springs and canals in winter.
    • Flamingos and wading birds such as herons and spoonbills feed in the shallows.

    Wildlife has been discussed after the trailers, but we have not verified specific animals against an official source, so this list describes real Florida only.

    How to use this page

    Inspiration is not a map

    Rockstar builds fictional places that echo real ones, then compresses, renames and exaggerates them for satire. A real landmark in Florida does not mean an identical copy exists in the game, and Rockstar has not published a map size or district list. Questions like these stay on what is still unknown until there is an official answer.

    The series has always worked this way. Liberty City echoes New York and San Andreas echoes California and Nevada, with invented names that leave room for parody of real brands, politics and media. That habit, and the attention it draws, is part of why GTA 6 matters to so many people.

    • Confirmed game names: Leonida (state) and Vice City (main city).
    • Real-world model: Florida, with Vice City reflecting Miami.
    • Not published: map size, full region list verified by us.
