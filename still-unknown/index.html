What we still do not know about GTA 6

Rockstar shares little. This page lists the open questions as of 14 November 2026, so you can spot rumours that pretend to answer them.

Updated: November 14, 2026

A good way to avoid misinformation is to know where the gaps are. The table below lists questions that people search for and the honest status of each one. If a site gives a firm answer to an item marked unknown, ask for the official source.

Platforms: PC, PS4 and PS5 Pro

The only confirmed platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. People often cite "PC in 2027" or "PC in 2028", but these are speculation and we do not publish them. A PC announcement would come from Rockstar. Likewise, a PS5 Pro mention appears at only one outlet and is not backed by an official enhancement list. See editions explained for how this affects buying.

Content and rating details

The ESRB descriptors are known, but PEGI has not been verified. Read ratings and parental guidance for what to do meanwhile. We also do not know what the "in-game purchases" label refers to.

Gameplay and world details

Rockstar's page confirms two protagonists, radio stations and an open world centred on Vice City. Trailers have shown other things, such as wildlife and social-media parodies, but we did not re-verify them against an official source, so we treat them as trailer content without detailed claims. The names of other regions outside Vice City appear in outlets, but we did not confirm them, so we use only Leonida and Vice City. A map size comparison with GTA V has not been published. The character switching question is explained in single-player vs online.

Release logistics

The release date, 19 November 2026, is confirmed. Less certain are the unlock time in your region and the preload start of 12 November 2026, which is reported by one outlet. Because Rockstar has not announced one global time, check your store page rather than a social media post. If you have a launch-day update in mind, assume nothing about its size.

Business details

Take-Two reaffirmed the date on 9 November 2026 and kept its net bookings guidance at US$8.0 to 8.2 billion. It did not publish pre-order counts or sales forecasts for the game itself. The context is in why GTA 6 matters.

How questions leave this page

  1. Rockstar or Take-Two publishes an answer on an official page or filing.
  2. We check the primary source and note the date.
  3. The item moves to "confirmed" on the relevant page, with the date in the text.

Reports from outlets are labelled reported until a primary source exists; the labels are explained in how we verify facts.

This list reflects our sources as of 14 November 2026. Some answers may already exist on pages we did not read, so check Rockstar's page first.

Questions to treat with caution after launch

After 19 November 2026, new claims will multiply: review scores, sales records, hours played, secrets. Wait for named outlets or Take-Two filings. Early "record sales" posts without a named source are marketing talk, not data. You can use the glossary to decode jargon in such posts, and the home explainer for the baseline facts.

Why unknowns are normal

Many items on this list will only be answered by a store listing, a patch note or a rating board publication. For a game of this size, such details often appear close to release or after it. An empty answer is not a sign of a problem. It means the publisher has not spoken yet, and the correct move is to wait for the primary source rather than accept a guess.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a GTA 6 PC release date?

No. A PC version has not been announced as of 14 November 2026.

Does GTA 6 have a PS5 Pro version?

No official PS5 Pro details were found. Rockstar’s page reportedly says the game plays best on PS5.

What is the GTA 6 PEGI rating?

Not verified. The ESRB rating shown on Rockstar’s page is Mature.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has not published a map size. Any figure online is a guess.

How many hours is GTA 6?

Story length has not been announced.

What is the GTA 6 file size?

We could not verify a file size. Check your store listing before preloading.

Is there a GTA 6 beta or demo?

None has been announced.

Why does Rockstar share so little?

Rockstar has not explained; the studio is known for limited communication, which is why rumours spread.
