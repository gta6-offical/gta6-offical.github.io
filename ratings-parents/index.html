GTA 6 age rating and guidance for parents

GTA 6 is rated Mature by the ESRB. This guide explains the descriptors in plain language and the practical steps parents can take on PS5 and Xbox.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    If a teenager has asked you for GTA 6, you probably want a straight answer. The game is rated for mature audiences, and this page shows what that means and which console tools can help. It is general guidance, not a review of the game.

    What the rating says

    Age ratings for Grand Theft Auto VI, as of 14 November 2026
    SystemRatingStatus
    ESRB (US and Canada)MatureDisplayed on Rockstar's official page

    The ESRB descriptors shown on Rockstar's page are blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, sexual content and use of drugs and alcohol. One outlet reports the rating was published on 6 October 2026; we treat that date as reported only.

    What the descriptors mean in practice

    Blood and gore, intense violence

    Expect graphic violence in missions and in the open world. Younger players should not play.

    Strong language and mature humor

    Frequent swearing and adult jokes, which are part of the series' satire.

    Nudity and sexual content

    Adult themes are present. The rating does not say how often or how explicit; we have not played the game.

    Drugs and alcohol

    Characters use drugs and alcohol in the story. Depiction is not endorsement.

    "Mature" means what age?

    The ESRB describes Mature as content generally suitable for ages 17 and up. That is the board's general definition, not something specific to this game. PEGI uses different age bands and is not verified for GTA 6, so do not rely on social media posts claiming a PEGI number. Every main GTA game has carried a mature rating; the series history shows why the controversy is old news.

    What are "in-game purchases"?

    The label means the game offers purchases of extra content or currency for real money. Rockstar has not explained what those are in GTA VI, and no online mode is announced, so we cannot say how large they are. Set a spending limit regardless. For the current state of online features, see single-player vs online.

    Parental controls on PS5 and Xbox

    A practical checklist

    1. Create a child account on the console, linked to your family or parent account.
    2. Set an age limit for games, so a Mature title cannot start under that account.
    3. Restrict purchases: require approval, set a monthly spending cap, or keep the child's wallet at zero.
    4. Manage playtime: both PlayStation and Xbox offer play-time schedules in their family tools.
    5. Check communication settings: if an online mode ever appears, review chat and friend requests before allowing it.

    Where to find the settings

    On PlayStation 5 the controls sit under Family and Parental Controls in the console settings, and can also be managed from a parent's account in the PlayStation app or website. On Xbox Series X|S they are managed through the Xbox Family Settings app or the family area of a Microsoft account. Menu names change with system updates, so use the console's own help pages for the exact steps. Other console basics, such as storage and system updates, are on the console preparation page, and comfort and display options are on our accessibility settings guide.

    Tip: talk with your child about why a game is rated Mature. A rating works best alongside a conversation and clear household rules.

    Gifts and shared consoles

    If you buy the game as a gift, the edition does not change the rating; see editions explained. On a shared console, a Mature game can appear in everyone's library, so set the age limit on each child's account, not just on yours.

    Making the decision

    Should my child play it?

    That is a family decision. Our editorial position is simple: the ESRB Mature rating is the official signal, so GTA 6 is not intended for children. If a teenager is close to 17, read independent reviews published after release and, if you can, watch some gameplay yourself. Independent reviews will describe the actual content in more detail than any label.

    Questions to ask before you decide

    • Has my child played other Mature-rated games, and how did we handle it?
    • Do I know which friends play it and how they talk about it?
    • Are console time limits and purchase approvals already set up?
    • Is there a shared family space where the game would be played?

    These questions start a household conversation. They are not a rating system, and no checklist replaces your judgement about a particular child.

    Scams that target young players

    Teenagers are a common target for fake "free GTA 6" offers, fake giveaways and phishing messages that ask for console logins. Our guide on avoiding GTA 6 scams is worth reading together.

    Where to read the official label

    The most reliable source is the rating on Rockstar's own page at rockstargames.com/VI and on your platform's store listing. Ratings can be updated, so look at the date. Open rating questions are kept in what is still unknown.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1What is the GTA 6 age rating?

    The ESRB rating shown on Rockstar’s official page is Mature. We have not verified a PEGI rating.

    Q2Is GTA 6 PEGI 18?

    Not verified. Do not rely on posts claiming a PEGI number until it appears on pegi.info or an official store page.

    Q3What does the GTA 6 rating say about content?

    Descriptors listed are blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, sexual content and use of drugs and alcohol.

    Q4Can a 13-year-old play GTA 6?

    The Mature rating means it is not intended for that age. The ESRB generally describes Mature as 17 and up. The decision is for parents, but the official guidance is clear.

    Q5Does GTA 6 have in-game purchases?

    The rating label includes in-game purchases. Rockstar has not detailed them for GTA VI.

    Q6How can I block GTA 6 on my child's account?

    Use the age-limit setting in the family tools of PS5 or Xbox so Mature games cannot launch under the child’s account.

    Q7Can parental controls limit spending?

    Yes. PlayStation and Xbox both let parents require approval or set spending limits for child accounts.

    Q8When was the GTA 6 rating published?

    One outlet reports 6 October 2026, but this is reported only.
