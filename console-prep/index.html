Setup & Safety

Getting your console ready for Grand Theft Auto VI

GTA 6 runs on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Here is a practical checklist for storage, updates, accounts and your connection, without guessing at a file size.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    Grand Theft Auto VI is released on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Most launch-day frustration has nothing to do with the game itself: a full drive, a pending system update or a payment card that fails. This page walks through the console side so the download and first launch go smoothly.

    What we do not know: Rockstar has not published a GTA VI file size that we could verify, and no console "system requirements" exist beyond the platform list. Any exact gigabyte figure you see on social media should be checked against your own store listing, which shows the size before you download.

    Storage: how much room do you really have?

    Console boxes list the raw drive size, but part of it is reserved for the operating system. The figures below are the general hardware specifications published by Sony and Microsoft, not anything specific to GTA 6.

    Free up space the sensible way

    • Open the storage screen in Settings and sort installed games by size. Large games you have finished are the obvious candidates.
    • Remember that deleting a digital game does not delete the licence. You can download it again later from your library.
    • Check saved video clips and screenshots. Captures pile up quietly, especially on PS5.
    • Save data is small and usually backed up to the cloud if you have a subscription, but confirm before deleting a game you might return to.

    Expansion drives and USB drives

    On PS5, a compatible M.2 NVMe SSD installed in the internal slot works exactly like the built-in drive, so you can play PS5 games from it. Sony's support pages list the supported sizes and recommend a heatsink. A USB drive on PS5 can store PS5 games and play PS4 games, but PS5 games must be copied back to internal or M.2 storage before you play them.

    On Xbox Series X|S, the Storage Expansion Card behaves like internal storage. A regular USB drive can play older Xbox One titles, but games built for Series X|S have to run from the internal drive or the expansion card. GTA VI is a current-generation game, so plan on internal or expansion storage for playing it.

    Updates, downloads and the first launch

    Update the console first

    New releases often expect the latest system software. Install any pending console update a few days before 19 November 2026 rather than on release night, when servers are busiest. On both consoles, automatic system updates can be switched on in Settings.

    Let downloads run while the console sleeps

    PS5 calls its low-power state Rest Mode; Xbox offers a sleep or standby power mode. Both can keep downloading game files and patches while the screen is off, as long as the relevant option is enabled. Turn on automatic game updates as well, because big games commonly receive a patch at or near launch. That is a general industry habit, not a Rockstar announcement for GTA VI. When and how pre-loading works is explained on our pre-order and preload guide.

    Wired beats wireless for big downloads

    1. If you can, connect the console to the router with an Ethernet cable.
    2. Run the console's built-in network test to see your download speed.
    3. Pause other large downloads in the house, such as PC game updates or cloud backups.
    4. If the speed is very low on launch day, the problem may be store traffic rather than your connection; try again later.

    Accounts, region and payment

    Check the account that owns the game

    A digital copy is tied to the account that bought it. On a shared console, make sure you buy with the account that will play, or that the console is set as the primary or home console for that account, so other profiles can use the licence. If the console is shared with children, set up family settings before release; our ratings and parental guidance page explains the Mature rating and spending limits.

    Region and payment basics

    Store accounts are linked to a country, which sets the currency and the price you see. Do not create an account in another region to chase a lower price: it can break payment methods and may conflict with store terms. Update an expired card or add wallet funds through the official store before launch week. Reported prices are covered in editions explained, and fake "cheap key" offers in our guide to avoiding GTA 6 scams.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1How big is the GTA 6 download?

    We could not verify a file size from Rockstar. Your store listing shows the size before you download, so check it there and leave extra room for updates.

    Q2Can I play GTA 6 from an external USB drive?

    On PS5, USB drives can store PS5 games but not play them. On Xbox Series X|S, current-generation games must run from internal storage or a Storage Expansion Card.

    Q3Is the PS5 internal drive big enough for GTA 6?

    The original PS5 has an 825 GB SSD with roughly 667 GB usable. Without a verified file size, the safest plan is to free space generously or add an M.2 SSD.

    Q4Will GTA 6 run on Xbox Series S?

    Yes. Xbox Series X|S is a confirmed platform. Series S has less storage, 512 GB on the base model, so plan space carefully.

    Q5Do I need to update my console before GTA 6?

    It is wise to install pending system updates a few days before 19 November 2026, because new games often expect the latest system software.

    Q6Can my console download GTA 6 while it is off?

    In PS5 Rest Mode or the Xbox sleep power mode, downloads and updates can continue if the option is enabled in Settings.

    Q7Can other users on my console play my digital copy?

    Usually yes if the console is set as the primary or home console of the buying account. Check your platform’s help pages for the exact rules.
