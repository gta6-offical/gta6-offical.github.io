Contents

    Grand Theft Auto VI, usually written GTA 6 or GTA VI, is the next main game in Rockstar Games' open-world crime series. If you have not followed the news, this page gives you the basics in plain English, and every claim is labelled by how well it is sourced.

    What is GTA 6 in one paragraph?

    GTA 6 is a single-player-focused action-adventure game set in the fictional US state of Leonida, with Vice City as its main city. Rockstar describes it with the line "Vice City, USA. The darkest side of the sunniest place in America." You play two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, whose story starts when "an easy score goes wrong" and spirals into criminal conspiracies. It is developed by Rockstar Games and published by Take-Two Interactive. The release date is 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    • Release date: 19 November 2026 (confirmed by Rockstar and Take-Two).
    • Platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been announced as of 14 November 2026.
    • Setting: State of Leonida, main city Vice City.
    • Protagonists: Jason and Lucia.

    Why is everyone talking about GTA 6?

    Three things combine. First, the wait: the previous main game, Grand Theft Auto V, came out in 2013, and GTA V has reportedly shipped more than 225 million copies as of the December 2025 quarter. Few entertainment products stay on sale and in the news for that long. Second, the release date moved: according to reports, the game was first aimed at Fall 2025, then 26 May 2026, then 19 November 2026. Rockstar's reported statement said the extra months would allow the team to finish the game "with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve". Third, the trailers broke records for views, according to reports. Our page on why GTA 6 matters goes into the business and cultural side.

    Where and when is it set?

    The setting is the state of Leonida, a fictional take on Florida, and its main city is Vice City, a fictional take on Miami. Vice City already appeared in the series: it was the setting of Grand Theft Auto: Vice City in 2002. GTA 6 is a new version of that idea rather than a remake, and Rockstar has not described it as a sequel to the 2002 plot. Vice City also appeared as one of three cities in the 1997 original and in the 2006 prequel Vice City Stories; the full story is on Vice City then and now, and the wider timeline on how the GTA series got here.

    Rockstar's website lists other regions, but this site only names Leonida and Vice City because those are the places we have verified against the official page. The same applies to the map: we do not publish a size comparison, because Rockstar has not given one. What we can describe is the real state that inspired the setting: Miami Beach's Art Deco streets, the Everglades, the Florida Keys and a climate with a hurricane season. That real-world background, clearly labelled as such, is on Leonida's Florida roots.

    Who are Jason and Lucia?

    Rockstar confirms that Jason and Lucia are the two protagonists. Outlets such as GamesRadar report their full names as Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, taken from Rockstar's website update in 2025, and report that Lucia is the first female lead in a main GTA game. The official page frames the story as two people pulled into a crime conspiracy after a job goes wrong. Whether and how you will switch between the two characters has not been confirmed to us, so we do not describe it.

    Which platforms can play it?

    Only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S are listed by Rockstar and Take-Two. Rockstar's page reportedly says the game plays best on PlayStation 5, but no PS5 Pro enhancement details have been confirmed. PlayStation 4 and Xbox One versions have not been announced, and neither has a PC version. Any claim about a PC date is speculation. The page on what is still unknown keeps a running list of these gaps.

    What do you actually get when you buy it?

    There are two editions named by Rockstar, Standard and Ultimate, plus a pre-order bonus called the Vintage Vice City Pack. Reported prices are US$79.99 for Standard and US$99.99 for Ultimate; we have not seen them on an official store page, so check the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or Rockstar Games Store before you pay. Reports also say a boxed copy contains a download code instead of a disc. Everything about the choice between editions, with a comparison table, is on editions explained. Pre-orders reportedly opened on 25 June 2026 and digital preload reportedly began on 12 November 2026; how ordering, preloading and launch-day unlocking work is explained in the pre-order and preload guide.

    Is it suitable for children?

    No, not for young children. The ESRB rating shown on Rockstar's page is Mature, with descriptors for blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, sexual content and use of drugs and alcohol, plus in-game purchases. We have not verified a PEGI rating, so we do not state one. Parents should read the ratings and parental guidance page for the practical steps: console parental controls, spending limits and what the "in-game purchases" label means.

    Is there multiplayer?

    No GTA VI online mode has been announced as of 14 November 2026. Outlets report that the PlayStation Store FAQ calls the game a single-player experience, which led to commentary about the future of GTA Online, the multiplayer mode of GTA V. There is no official statement about GTA Online ending that we could find. See single-player vs online for a careful reading of what is and is not known.

    Which words will you hear?

    Fans use shorthand such as "GTA VI", "Leonida", "Vintage Vice City Pack", "preload" and "open world". If a term in a headline confuses you, the GTA glossary explains it in a sentence or two.

    How to tell reliable GTA 6 news from rumours

    Because the game is so popular, many pages publish guesses as facts. A few quick habits help. Prefer Rockstar's own page and Take-Two's investor filings. Treat a single outlet quoting an unnamed source as unconfirmed. Be suspicious of leaked maps, "system requirements" for consoles, or exact sales figures before the release. Our how we verify facts page lists the labels used on this site: confirmed, reported and unknown. Our team is an independent fan editorial team; see about this site for the disclaimer and privacy details.

    Beware scams. Fake "free download", "PC version" and "early access beta" offers circulate around every big launch. No demo, beta or PC version has been announced, and the only places to buy are the official storefronts and retailers. This site does not link to any download. The common tricks, and how to secure your PlayStation or Microsoft account, are in how to avoid GTA 6 scams.

    Getting your console ready

    Whether you bought a digital copy or a boxed one, the game has to be installed before you can play, and big games need a lot of free space. Rockstar has not published a file size we could verify, so check the figure on your store listing.

    Storage and updates

    The original PlayStation 5 ships with an 825 GB SSD and later models with more; the Xbox Series S starts at 512 GB and the Series X at 1 TB. Both consoles can add fast expansion storage, but current-generation games cannot be played from an ordinary USB drive. Update the console's system software before release day and allow downloads in rest mode. The step-by-step version is on our console preparation guide.

    Comfort and accessibility

    Rockstar has not announced GTA VI's own accessibility options. Your console already offers system-wide tools such as larger text, high contrast, button remapping, captions and adaptive controllers, which apply to every game. They are explained, console by console, in our accessibility and settings guide.
