GTA 6 single-player vs online: where things stand

No GTA VI online mode has been announced. Here is what is reported, what is confirmed, and what the status of GTA Online means for you.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    People often ask whether GTA 6 has multiplayer. The accurate answer as of 14 November 2026 is that no online mode has been announced, and outlets report the game is described as a single-player experience. This page explains what that wording does and does not tell you.

    What "single-player" means here

    A single-player game is one you play alone, following a story at your own pace. Reports quote the PlayStation Store FAQ calling GTA VI a single-player experience, and outlets such as GamesRadar and PC Gamer wrote that an online mode looks unlikely at launch. We could not read the full original text of those reports, so we describe it as reported. The wording leaves room for later additions, and Rockstar has not said either way.

    What is the story mode?

    Rockstar's page describes two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, in the state of Leonida, and a story that begins when "an easy score goes wrong" and leads into criminal conspiracies. The page also mentions in-game radio stations and an open world centred on Vice City. There is no confirmed story length, mission count or ending structure; those are on what is still unknown.

    GTA Online and connection questions

    What happens to GTA Online?

    GTA Online is the multiplayer mode of the previous game, GTA V, launched in 2013. It continues to exist, and we found no official statement that it will close. It is separate from GTA VI: nothing announced says progress, characters or purchases carry over into the new game. If you play GTA Online now, nothing in the sources we use says you must stop. Its background is on how the GTA series got here.

    Do you need an internet connection?

    Rockstar has not published online requirements for GTA VI. Console subscriptions such as PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass Core are normally needed for online multiplayer on consoles, but because no online mode is announced, we cannot say whether any would be needed. We also cannot say whether the first launch requires a connection. A download is needed in any case for digital copies and, reportedly, for boxed copies that contain a code. Launch-day updates are common for big releases, which is a general habit rather than a Rockstar statement. Practical download planning is on the console preparation page.

    Typical rumours to ignore

    Search results often claim "GTA Online 2 is confirmed", list online features, promise a "multiplayer beta" sign-up or show a leaked player count. None of that is in Rockstar's materials. Beta sign-up pages are a known way to steal logins; see how to avoid GTA 6 scams. Claims that you can switch characters with a button press come from one outlet snippet and are unverified, so we do not state them.

    Why it matters, and what would change this page

    • For buyers: do not pay extra for an edition on the assumption that it includes online access. See editions explained.
    • For parents: an online mode would bring chat with strangers. None is announced, but the rating label mentions in-game purchases; see ratings and parental guidance.
    • For players with specific needs: a solo game lets you pause and play at your own pace, which matters for comfort settings covered in our accessibility guide.
    • For fans: Rockstar could announce updates or modes at any time, so check official channels instead of rumours.

    How to read future announcements

    If Rockstar announces a multiplayer mode, look for three things: a name, a platform list and a date. Be careful with "coming soon" posts without a source. An online mode could also bring its own purchases and communication features, so parents would need to revisit their console settings.

    What would make us update

    We will revise this page if Rockstar or Take-Two announce an online mode, other modes, or a closing date for GTA Online. Until then the correct reading is cautious: story-focused, no online mode announced. Compare with the official site at rockstargames.com/VI.

    Short version: GTA VI is reported as single-player; no multiplayer is announced; GTA Online for GTA V has not been officially said to end.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1Does GTA 6 have multiplayer?

    No online mode has been announced as of 14 November 2026. Outlets report it is described as a single-player experience.

    Q2Is there GTA Online 2?

    Not announced. Claims of a confirmed GTA Online 2 are not backed by Rockstar’s materials.

    Q3Will GTA Online for GTA V shut down?

    We found no official statement that it will end.

    Q4Can I play GTA 6 co-op?

    No co-op or split-screen mode has been announced.

    Q5Can I play GTA 6 offline?

    Rockstar has not published online requirements for GTA VI, so we cannot say. You need a connection to download digital copies.

    Q6Do I need PlayStation Plus for GTA 6?

    No online mode is announced, so no subscription requirement has been stated. This could change if an online mode is announced.

    Q7Does GTA Online progress transfer to GTA 6?

    Nothing announced says so. GTA Online belongs to GTA V and is separate from GTA VI.

    Q8How long is the GTA 6 story?

    Rockstar has not said, so any figure you see is a guess.
