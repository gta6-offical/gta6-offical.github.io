Game & Buying

GTA 6 editions explained: what you actually get

Rockstar names two editions, Standard and Ultimate, and a pre-order bonus. Prices and extras are mostly reported, so here is what is solid and what to check.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    Buying a big game can be confusing. This page explains the GTA 6 editions in plain terms, separates official names from reported details and lists what to check before you pay. Ordering and download timing have their own page, the pre-order and preload guide.

    Standard, Ultimate and the bonus pack

    The short answer

    Rockstar's official page names two editions of Grand Theft Auto VI, Standard and Ultimate, and a pre-order bonus called the Vintage Vice City Pack. Outlets, including The National, report prices of US$79.99 for Standard and US$99.99 for Ultimate. We have not seen those prices on an official store page, so use them as a guide only. The game releases on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    The honest summary: Rockstar confirms the names, while the contents of each edition and the exact prices should be read from the store you plan to use. We do not list extra items for Ultimate because we could not verify them. Prices in other countries and currencies are not covered by our sources.

    The Vintage Vice City Pack

    The Vintage Vice City Pack is the confirmed name of the pre-order bonus. According to The National's report, it includes a 1955 Vapid Stanier car, outfits and weapon designs, and was offered for orders placed before 20 November. A single aggregator also mentions one month of GTA+ with a digital pre-order; we treat that as unverified. Because the pre-order window may have closed by the time you read this, check whether the pack is still listed. The name nods to the city's past, explained on Vice City then and now.

    Format, platform and price questions

    Boxed copy or digital?

    The National reports that the boxed copy contains a download code rather than a game disc. We could not confirm that on a store page, so read the product description before you buy a boxed copy. If a physical disc matters to you, for example to resell or lend it later, check what is inside the box. If you prefer digital, buy directly from the console store so the game is tied to your account. Either way you will need free storage space; plan it with our console preparation guide.

    PC, PS4, PS5 Pro or a demo?

    No PC version, demo, beta, PlayStation 4 or Xbox One version has been announced as of 14 November 2026. Rockstar's page reportedly says the game plays best on PlayStation 5, but we found no official PS5 Pro enhancement list. These gaps are tracked on what is still unknown.

    What to do if the price differs

    Store prices vary by country, tax and currency, and may change. If the price you see differs from the reported US figures, trust the official store. Retailers also differ in what they include with a pre-order, so read the small print and keep the order confirmation until the game is working. A big discount on a brand-new game from an unofficial seller is a warning sign; the common tricks are listed in how to avoid GTA 6 scams.

    Buying it as a gift

    Digital gifting options differ by store, and a digital copy is tied to the account that redeems it, so check that the person uses the same platform and region as the store you buy from. A boxed copy avoids the account question, but if it holds a download code, as reported, the code still needs an internet connection and storage space on the recipient's console.

    Does the edition affect online play?

    No GTA VI online mode has been announced, so no edition is described as unlocking one. The rating label mentions in-game purchases, but Rockstar has not explained what they are. See single-player vs online.

    Before you pay

    1. Confirm your console: PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S. The two versions are not interchangeable.
    2. Open the official store listing and read the edition name and price in your currency.
    3. Read what the listing says about the pre-order bonus and whether it is still offered.
    4. For a boxed copy, check whether it holds a disc or a code.
    5. Check the age rating and spending settings if the console is shared with a child, as explained in ratings and parental guidance.

    Warning: keys at suspiciously low prices, "free download" offers and anything asking for your console login are scams or break store terms. This site links to no such offers. Use official storefronts.

    Verdict: if you only want the game and are unsure, start from Standard and read the official listing for what Ultimate adds. Do not pay extra on the basis of rumours. Official information is at rockstargames.com/VI.
