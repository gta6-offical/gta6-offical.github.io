About this site

This is an unofficial, independent fan site that explains Grand Theft Auto VI in plain English.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    This site is run by an independent fan editorial team. It is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names are trademarks of their owners.

    What we do

    We explain GTA 6 for people who have not followed it: what it is, the series and its settings, editions, ordering, console preparation, ratings, accessibility, online status, scams and open questions. Start with the main explainer or read how we verify facts.

    Sources policy

    Official Rockstar and Take-Two sources come first. Reports from outlets are attributed, and unverified claims are left out. Series history and real Florida geography are labelled as background, not as game content. Facts are current as of 14 November 2026; open items are on what is still unknown. Official site: rockstargames.com/VI.

    Privacy

    • The hosting server keeps ordinary server logs.
    • We use Ahrefs Web Analytics, which gives aggregate statistics without cookies.
    • We set no cookies of our own and have no accounts or comment forms.
    • We do not sell or collect personal data.

    Contact

    Contact is via the site owner. We do not publish an email address on the site. For corrections, mention the page and the claim.

    Please note

    We do not offer downloads, cracks, cheats for online play or gambling content, and we link to none. Nobody from this site will ever message you with a "beta" or a free copy; see avoiding scams. For families, read ratings and parental guidance.
