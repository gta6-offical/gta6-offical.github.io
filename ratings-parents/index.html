GTA 6 age rating and guidance for parents

GTA 6 is rated Mature by the ESRB. This guide explains the descriptors in plain language and the practical steps parents can take on PS5 and Xbox.

Updated: November 14, 2026

If a teenager has asked you for GTA 6, you probably want a straight answer. The game is rated for mature audiences, and this page shows what that means and which console tools can help. It is general guidance, not a review of the game.

The ESRB descriptors shown on Rockstar's page are: blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, sexual content and use of drugs and alcohol. The label also includes "in-game purchases". One outlet reports the rating was published on 6 October 2026, but we treat that date as reported only.

What the descriptors mean in practice

Blood and gore, intense violence

Expect graphic violence in missions and in the open world. Younger players should not play.

Strong language and mature humor

Frequent swearing and adult jokes, which are part of the series' satire.

Nudity and sexual content

Adult themes are present. The rating does not tell you how often or how explicit; we have not played the game.

Drugs and alcohol

Characters use drugs and alcohol in the story. This is depicted, not endorsed by the rating.

"Mature" means what age?

ESRB describes Mature as content generally suitable for ages 17 and older. That is the board's general definition, not something specific to this game. PEGI uses different age bands and is not yet verified for GTA 6, so do not rely on social media posts claiming a PEGI number.

What are "in-game purchases"?

The label means the game offers purchases of extra content or currency for real money. Rockstar has not explained what those are in GTA VI, and no online mode is announced, so we cannot say how large they are. Set a spending limit regardless. For the current state of online features, see single-player vs online.

Parental controls: a practical checklist

  1. Create a child account on the console, linked to a family or parent account.
  2. Set an age limit for games in the console's family settings, so a Mature title cannot start under that account.
  3. Restrict purchases: require a password or set a monthly spending cap, or set the child's wallet to zero.
  4. Manage playtime: both PlayStation and Xbox offer play-time schedules in their family tools.
  5. Check chat and social features: if an online mode ever appears, review its communication settings before allowing it.

Menu names differ between PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and change with system updates, so use the console's own help pages for the exact steps.

Tip: talk with your child about why a game is rated Mature. A rating works best alongside a conversation and clear household rules.

Should my child play it?

That is a family decision. Our editorial position is simple: the ESRB Mature rating is the official signal, so GTA 6 is not intended for children. If a teenager is nearly 17, you might want to look at independent reviews after release and, if you can, watch some gameplay yourself. We cannot offer a review of the game because it has not been released at the time of writing.

Gifts and shared consoles

If you buy the game as a gift, the edition does not change the rating. See editions explained for how to buy from official stores. On a shared console, a Mature game can appear in everyone's library; use the age limit setting for children's accounts.

Where to read the official label

The most reliable source is the rating shown on Rockstar's own page at rockstargames.com/VI, and on the store listing of your platform. Ratings can be updated, so look at the date. Our approach to such labels is described in how we verify facts, and open rating questions are in what is still unknown.

For basics about the game, return to the GTA 6 explainer.

Questions to ask before you decide

  • Has my child seen other Mature-rated games, and how did we handle it?
  • Do I know which friends play it, and with whom my child would talk about it?
  • Are console time limits and purchase approvals already set up?
  • Is there a shared family space where the game would be played?

These questions are meant to start a household conversation. They are not a rating system, and no checklist can replace your own judgement about a particular child. Independent reviews written after release will give more detail about the actual content than any label.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GTA 6 age rating?

The ESRB rating shown on Rockstar’s official page is Mature. We have not verified a PEGI rating.

Is GTA 6 PEGI 18?

Not verified. Do not rely on posts claiming a PEGI number until it appears on pegi.info or an official store page.

What does the GTA 6 rating say about content?

Descriptors listed are blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, sexual content and use of drugs and alcohol.

Can a 13-year-old play GTA 6?

The Mature rating means it is not intended for that age. ESRB generally describes Mature as 17 and older. The decision is for parents, but the official guidance is clear.

Does GTA 6 have in-game purchases?

The rating label includes in-game purchases. Rockstar has not detailed them for GTA VI.

How can I block GTA 6 on my child's account?

Use the age-limit setting in the family tools of PS5 or Xbox so Mature games cannot launch, and restrict purchases.

Can parental controls limit spending?

Yes. PlayStation and Xbox both let parents require approval or set spending limits for child accounts.

When was the GTA 6 rating published?

One outlet reports 6 October 2026, but this is reported only.
