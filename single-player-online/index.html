GTA 6 single-player vs online: where things stand

No GTA VI online mode has been announced. Here is what is reported, what is confirmed, and what the status of GTA Online means for you.

Updated: November 14, 2026

People often ask whether GTA 6 has multiplayer. The accurate answer as of 14 November 2026 is: no online mode has been announced, and outlets report the game is described as a single-player experience.

What "single-player" means here

A single-player game is one you play alone, following a story at your own pace. Reports quote the PlayStation Store FAQ calling GTA VI a single-player experience, and outlets such as GamesRadar and PC Gamer wrote that an online mode looks unlikely at launch. We could not read the full original text of those reports, so we describe it as reported. The wording leaves room for later additions, and Rockstar has not said either way.

What is the story mode?

Rockstar's page describes two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, in the state of Leonida, and a story that begins when "an easy score goes wrong" and leads into criminal conspiracies. The page also mentions in-game radio stations and an open world centred on Vice City. We have no confirmed length for the story, number of missions or ending structure. Those are listed on what is still unknown.

What about GTA Online?

GTA Online is the multiplayer mode of the previous game, GTA V. It continues to exist, and we found no official statement that it will close. It is separate from GTA VI: progress, characters and purchases there do not carry into the new game as far as anything announced says. If you play GTA Online now, nothing in the sources we use says you must stop. For the history of that mode, read how the GTA series got here.

Do you need an internet connection?

Rockstar has not published information about online requirements for GTA VI. Console subscriptions such as PlayStation Plus or Xbox online membership are normally needed for online multiplayer, but because no online mode is announced, we cannot say whether any would be needed. We also cannot say whether the game requires an internet connection for the first launch or for updates. Expect a launch-day update, as is common for big releases, but that is a general habit and not a Rockstar statement.

Why does it matter?

  • For buyers: do not pay extra for an edition on the assumption that it includes online access. See editions explained.
  • For parents: an online mode would bring chat and strangers. As of today none is announced, but the rating label mentions in-game purchases; see ratings and parental guidance.
  • For fans: Rockstar could announce future updates or modes at any time, so check official channels instead of rumours.

Typical rumours to ignore

Search results often claim "GTA Online 2 is confirmed", list online features or show a leaked player count. None of that is in Rockstar's materials. Our verification page explains why we ignore single-source or screenshot-only claims. A related detail: reports that you can switch between characters with a button press come from one outlet snippet and are unverified, so we do not state them.

What would change this page?

We will update this page if Rockstar or Take-Two announce an online mode, additional modes, or a closing date for GTA Online. Until then the correct reading is cautious: story-focused, no online mode announced. Compare with the official site: rockstargames.com/VI.

Short version: GTA VI is reported as single-player; no multiplayer is announced; GTA Online for GTA V has not been officially said to end.

More background is in the GTA 6 explainer and the glossary.

How to read future announcements

If Rockstar announces a multiplayer mode later, look for three things: a name, a platform list and a date. Be careful with phrases such as "coming soon" without a source. Also remember that an online mode, if it ever appears, could carry its own rating notice and purchases, so parents would need to revisit their settings. Until then, the safest reading of the available sources is a story-focused game with no announced online component.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have multiplayer?

No online mode has been announced as of 14 November 2026. Outlets report it is described as a single-player experience.

Is there GTA Online 2?

Not announced. Claims of a confirmed GTA Online 2 are not backed by Rockstar’s materials.

Will GTA Online for GTA V shut down?

We found no official statement that it will end.

Can I play GTA 6 co-op?

No co-op or split-screen mode has been announced.

Can I play GTA 6 offline?

Rockstar has not published online requirements for GTA VI, so we cannot say.

Do I need PlayStation Plus for GTA 6?

No online mode is announced, so no subscription requirement has been stated. This could change if an online mode is announced.

Who are the playable characters?

Jason and Lucia are the confirmed protagonists in the story.

How long is the GTA 6 story?

Rockstar has not said, so any figure you see is a guess.
