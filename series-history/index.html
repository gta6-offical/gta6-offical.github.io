How the GTA series got to GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto is nearly three decades old. This page is a short history for newcomers, from the first top-down game to GTA VI.

Updated November 14, 2026

Contents

    You do not need to have played earlier games to enjoy a new Grand Theft Auto, because each main entry tells a separate story. Still, knowing the history explains why GTA VI is such a big deal, why Vice City sounds familiar and why the wait felt so long.

    Note on sourcing: the release years and names below are long-established public history, not items from the GTA VI fact sheet we use for news. GTA VI details are checked separately; see how we verify facts.

    There were also expansions and spin-offs, such as Grand Theft Auto: London 1969 (1999), Liberty City Stories (2005), Vice City Stories (2006), the two GTA IV episodes of 2009 and Chinatown Wars (2009). The table lists the games most people mean by "the series". Each main game has a self-contained plot, so you can start with GTA VI.

    A quick way to remember the eras

    • Top-down era (1997 to 1999): the original game and its sequel, seen from above, made by DMA Design, the Scottish studio later renamed Rockstar North.
    • 3D era (2001 to 2006): Grand Theft Auto III, Vice City and San Andreas defined the series most people recognise, on PlayStation 2 first.
    • HD era (2008 to 2013): Grand Theft Auto IV and V brought modern graphics, a more grounded tone and online play.
    • Current era (2026): Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    These labels are our own shorthand for readers, not official terms.

    Why the gap before GTA VI is unusual

    Between 1997 and 2013 a new main game appeared every one to five years. The 13 years between GTA V and GTA VI are the exception. During that time GTA V was re-released for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in 2014, for PC in 2015 and for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S in 2022, which kept it in the charts. The commercial side is on why GTA 6 matters.

    What stays the same and what changed

    The constants

    • An open world: a large city or region you can explore freely, on foot or in vehicles.
    • Story missions plus side activities: a main plot you can follow in order, and optional things to do.
    • Satire of modern life: radio, adverts and television inside the game poke fun at real culture.
    • A mature rating: violence, strong language and adult themes. Details in ratings and parental guidance.

    What GTA V changed

    GTA V let players switch between three protagonists during the story and, from October 2013, added GTA Online, a separate multiplayer mode that has been updated for years. Outlets report that GTA V reached 225 million units shipped by the December 2025 quarter, and the franchise more than 465 million. That success is part of why the follow-up took so long and why expectations are so high.

    Where Vice City fits

    Vice City was one of the three cities in the 1997 original and then the setting of the 2002 game Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. GTA VI uses the name again as the main city of the state of Leonida. This is a new game with new characters, not a remake, and Rockstar has not confirmed any story connection to earlier plots. The full story of the city, from 1997 to today, is on Vice City then and now, and the real places behind the new state are described in Leonida's Florida roots.

    What is different in GTA VI, and how to catch up

    Two protagonists

    Jason and Lucia are confirmed. GTA V had three; earlier games usually had one.

    A return to Vice City

    The city is confirmed as the main location inside the state of Leonida.

    New hardware

    PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only, as of 14 November 2026.

    Single-player focus

    Reported as a single-player experience, with no online mode announced. See single-player vs online.

    Do I need to play older games first?

    No. Rockstar builds each main entry as a separate story. If you want a taste of the series first, GTA V is the most recent, but this is a personal choice rather than a requirement. Buy older games only from official stores.

    How critics and fans describe the series

    Fans praise the scale and detail of the worlds and the radio stations. Critics often point to the controversy around violence and the way each game satirises society. Both views are part of the series story, and each new game revives the debate. We do not give review scores for GTA VI. Terms such as "open world" and "sandbox" are explained in the GTA glossary.

    Comparisons to watch for

    After launch, people will compare GTA VI with GTA V. Comparisons about map size, number of missions or hours of play need official numbers, and none has been published by Rockstar. Those items sit on the unknowns page until they are answered. For the basics go back to the GTA 6 explainer, or check the official GTA VI site.

    Frequently asked questions

    Q1How many main GTA games are there?

    Counting numbered and major titles: Grand Theft Auto (1997), GTA 2 (1999), III (2001), Vice City (2002), San Andreas (2004), IV (2008), V (2013) and VI (19 November 2026).

    Q2Do I need to play GTA V before GTA 6?

    No. Each main game has its own story, so you can start with GTA VI.

    Q3When did GTA V come out?

    GTA V was released in 2013, with later versions for PS4 and Xbox One in 2014, PC in 2015 and PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in 2022.

    Q4Is GTA 6 a Vice City remake?

    No. It uses Vice City as the main city in the new state of Leonida with new protagonists, Jason and Lucia.

    Q5What is GTA Online?

    It is the multiplayer mode of GTA V, launched in 2013. It is separate from GTA VI, and no GTA VI online mode has been announced.

    Q6Which GTA game sold the most?

    Outlets report GTA V at 225 million units shipped as of the December 2025 quarter, and the series at over 465 million.

    Q7Who makes the GTA games?

    Rockstar Games develops them and Take-Two Interactive publishes them. The 1997 original was made by DMA Design, later Rockstar North.

    Q8Why did GTA 6 take so long?

    Rockstar has not given a full explanation. Reported delays moved the game from Fall 2025 to 19 November 2026, after GTA V had stayed on sale for 13 years.
